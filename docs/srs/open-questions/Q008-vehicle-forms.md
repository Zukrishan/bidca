# Q008 — Vehicles: buyer checklist, seller disclosure, or both?
Status: **Decided — provisional default (26 Oct 2026)**, chosen by us so building can continue; the client may change it, and a change is just a new decision row in `docs/ASK-NOTES.md`.

**Both:**
- **Buyer inspection checklist** — downloadable PDF on every Vehicles listing and its transaction page, no login. Placeholder content until the client sends the real checklist; no VIN/listing auto-fill for now.
- **Seller disclosure** — required fields when listing in Vehicles: accident history (yes/no + description), known mechanical issues (yes/no + description), number of previous owners, inspection/safety certificate (yes/no, optional upload), odometer reading, liens or loans outstanding (yes/no). Shown on the listing page.
