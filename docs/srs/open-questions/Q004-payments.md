# Q004 — Payments / fees
Status: **Decided — provisional default (26 Oct 2026)**, chosen by us so building can continue; the client may change it, and a change is just a new decision row in `docs/ASK-NOTES.md`.

- Transactions: settled by the source — BidCa never touches, holds, refunds or moves money (NFR-07).
- Paid featured listings, premium members and listing fees: **not built** for launch; they stay in "later" (FR-35) until the client asks.
- The current "Featured" home section is chosen by bid count, not paid. It is **renamed "Popular"** so "Featured" can later mean a paid placement without confusing users.
