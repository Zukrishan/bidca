# Q015 — Launch plan: 3 months free, 30 listings a month
Status: **Decided — provisional default (26 Oct 2026)**, chosen by us so building can continue; the client may change it, and a change is just a new decision row in `docs/ASK-NOTES.md`.

- The free period is **3 months from the public launch date** (a setting), not per user.
- The cap is **30 listings per user per calendar month (Pacific time)**, counting every listing submitted for approval, including relists; drafts and rejected listings do not count.
- After the free period nothing changes automatically: the cap stays and nothing is charged until Q004 is answered.
